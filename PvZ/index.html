🌻 Plants vs Zombies 🧟

Defend your lawn from the zombie horde!

Choose Level

How to Play

  • Hover over ☀ sun to auto-collect it
  • Click a plant card, then click a grid cell to plant
  • Peashooters shoot zombies · Sunflowers make sun
  • Twin Sunflower (150☀) — place on a Sunflower for double sun production
  • Puff-shroom (0☀) — free short-range spores; Shroom Pult (0☀) — free puff-shroom that lobs spores the full lane; Scaredy Shroom (25☀) — full-range spores but ducks underground when zombies get close; Sun-shroom (25☀) — grows to produce more sun
  • Mushrooms are awake on Night and Foggy Pool levels; they sleep during the day
  • Coffee Bean (75☀) — place on a sleeping mushroom to wake it on day/pool/roof levels
  • Hypno-shroom (75☀) — when awake, hypnotizes the zombie that eats it; it then fights for you (Gargantuars and Giga-gargantuars are immune). Wake it with Coffee Bean before zombies arrive on day/pool/roof levels
  • Gatling Pea (400☀) — place on a Peashooter for rapid-fire peas
  • Torchwood (175☀) — peas that pass through become fire peas (2× damage)
  • Wall-nuts block zombies · Tall-nut (125☀) is a taller, tougher wall (upgradeable)
  • Cherry Bombs explode
  • Jalapeno (125☀) — instantly burns every zombie in its row
  • Ice-shroom (75☀) — freezes all zombies on screen for 4s when planted
  • Squash (50☀) — waits for a zombie, then jumps and crushes it
  • Potato Mines bury underground, arm in 7s, then detonate on contact
  • Snow Peas slow zombies with icy projectiles
  • Doom Shroom (5000☀) — wipes all zombies, leaves a 2-min crater
  • Boom Shroom (125☀) — sleeps during day; wake with Coffee Bean to detonate and wipe all non-boss zombies (bosses take 1 instant-kill hit)
  • Lily Pad (25☀, Pool only) — place on water, then plant on top
  • Sea-shroom (0☀, Pool only) — free short-range spores; place directly in water
  • Tangle Kelp (25☀, Pool only) — place in water; drags one zombie under (not Gargantuars)
  • Spikeweed (100☀, Pool only) — damages zombies that walk over it; pops one All-Star helmet then breaks
  • Spikerock (125☀) — place on any lane, or on Spikeweed to upgrade it; tougher spikes that pop 9 All-Star helmets
  • On pool levels, only basic zombies swim — they wear ducky tubes in the water rows
  • Foggy Pool — nighttime backyard pool with no sky sun; fog hides zombies and creeps one column closer each wave
  • Plantern (25☀) — clears fog in the tiles around it; Blover (100☀) — blows away all fog for 8 seconds
  • Flower Pot (25☀, Roof only) — required before planting on roof tiles
  • Roof levels — the slope blocks Peashooters & Snow Peas; use Cabbage-pult & Kernel-pult to lob over it
  • Kernel-pult — lobs kernels; butter hits stun zombies
  • Cob Cannon (500☀) — place on two adjacent Kernel-pults; click it, then click a target to fire a cob explosion (upgradeable: faster reload, tougher, wider blast, more boss hits)
  • Melon-pult (300☀) — lobs melons with splash damage to nearby zombies
  • Winter Melon (200☀) — place on a Melon-pult for faster icy melons that slow zombies
  • Click Upgrade, then click a plant to power it up (max level 3) — all plants except instant-use ones
  • A Flag Zombie marches in first — when it raises the flag, the wave begins!
  • Gargantuars smash plants — at half health they throw an Imp ahead of them
  • Giga-gargantuar (wave 12+) — even tougher, throws an Imp at half health too
  • Every 20 waves, Dr. Zomboss appears — defeat him to advance!
  • Dr ZomBOSS FIGHT — night roof level where Dr. Zomboss spawns every wave's zombies!
  • Don't let zombies reach your house!
